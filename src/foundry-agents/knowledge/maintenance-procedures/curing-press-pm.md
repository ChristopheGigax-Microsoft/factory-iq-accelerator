# Preventive Maintenance Procedure: Curing Press

## Scope
This procedure covers the routine preventive maintenance of hydraulic curing presses used in tire manufacturing.

## Schedule
- **Daily**: Visual inspection of hydraulic lines, check pressure gauges
- **Weekly**: Lubrication of moving parts, check mold alignment
- **Monthly**: Full hydraulic oil analysis, thermocouple calibration check
- **Quarterly**: Replace hydraulic filters, inspect seals and O-rings
- **Annually**: Full overhaul of hydraulic system, mold replacement assessment

## Safety Requirements
- Lock-out/Tag-out (LOTO) required before any maintenance
- Minimum 2 technicians for hydraulic work
- Required PPE: heat-resistant gloves, safety glasses, steel-toe boots
- Allow press to cool below 50°C before internal access

## Procedure Steps

### Daily Inspection (15 minutes)
1. Check hydraulic pressure gauge — nominal range: 140–160 bar
2. Inspect all visible hydraulic lines for leaks or bulging
3. Verify temperature display matches setpoint (±2°C)
4. Check safety interlock operation (door switch, pressure relief)
5. Log findings in maintenance system

### Weekly Lubrication (45 minutes)
1. Apply grease to guide columns (4 points, use Mobil XHP 222)
2. Check mold parallelism with dial indicator (max 0.05mm deviation)
3. Inspect heating platens for wear or damage
4. Verify cycle counter and compare to maintenance schedule

### Monthly Calibration Check (2 hours)
1. Take hydraulic oil sample for lab analysis
2. Calibrate all thermocouples using reference probe
3. Check hydraulic pump output pressure and flow rate
4. Inspect accumulator pre-charge pressure (should be 70% of system pressure)
5. Test all safety devices and emergency stops

## Troubleshooting Quick Reference

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Pressure drop > 10 bar | Hydraulic leak or pump wear | Inspect lines, check pump output |
| Temperature overshoot > 5°C | Thermocouple drift or heater relay stuck | Calibrate TC, check relay contacts |
| Cycle time increase > 10% | Hydraulic flow restriction | Check filters, inspect valves |
| Vibration increase | Bearing wear or misalignment | Vibration analysis, check alignment |

## Required Skills
- Hydraulics Level 2
- Electrical Safety Certification
- Thermocouple Calibration Training
