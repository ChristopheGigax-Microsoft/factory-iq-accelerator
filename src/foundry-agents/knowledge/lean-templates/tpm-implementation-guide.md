# TPM (Total Productive Maintenance) Implementation Guide

## Eight Pillars of TPM

### 1. Autonomous Maintenance
Operators take ownership of basic equipment care:
- Cleaning, inspection, lubrication (CIL)
- Detect abnormalities early
- Restore basic conditions

**Steps**:
1. Initial cleaning and inspection
2. Eliminate sources of contamination
3. Establish cleaning/lubrication standards
4. General inspection training
5. Autonomous inspection checklists
6. Workplace organization
7. Full autonomous management

### 2. Planned Maintenance
Systematic prevention of breakdowns:
- Time-based maintenance (TBM)
- Condition-based maintenance (CBM)
- Predictive maintenance using sensor data

**Key Metrics**:
- MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures)
- MTTR (Mean Time To Repair)
- Maintenance cost per unit produced

### 3. Focused Improvement (Kobetsu Kaizen)
Systematic elimination of losses:
- Select chronic/complex problems
- Apply structured problem-solving (PDCA, DMAIC)
- Target Six Big Losses
- Cross-functional team approach

### 4. Quality Maintenance
Zero defects through equipment conditions:
- Set conditions that prevent defects
- Monitor conditions vs. quality output
- Investigate any condition-quality correlation changes

### 5. Early Equipment Management
Build maintainability into new equipment:
- Life-cycle cost analysis
- Maintenance prevention design
- Commissioning and vertical startup support

### 6. Training and Education
Develop multi-skilled operators and technicians:
- Skills matrix and gap analysis
- OPL (One-Point Lessons) for knowledge sharing
- Certification levels for autonomous maintenance

### 7. Safety, Health, Environment
Zero accidents as foundation:
- Risk assessments for all maintenance tasks
- Near-miss reporting and trending
- Environmental impact reduction

### 8. Office TPM
Apply TPM principles to support functions:
- Reduce information flow losses
- Improve support process reliability

## OEE Calculation Reference

```
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

Availability = (Planned Time - Downtime) / Planned Time
Performance = (Actual Output × Ideal Cycle Time) / Available Time
Quality = Good Units / Total Units Produced
```
