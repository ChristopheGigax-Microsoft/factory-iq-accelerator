# Incoming Material Inspection Criteria

## Purpose
Defines acceptance criteria for raw materials used in tire manufacturing to ensure product quality and process stability.

## Rubber Compound Specifications

### Natural Rubber (NR)
| Parameter | Min | Max | Test Method |
|-----------|-----|-----|-------------|
| Mooney Viscosity (ML1+4 @ 100°C) | 60 | 80 | ASTM D1646 |
| Ash Content (%) | - | 0.5 | ASTM D5630 |
| Volatile Matter (%) | - | 0.8 | ASTM D5668 |
| Dirt Content (%) | - | 0.04 | ASTM D1278 |

### Synthetic Rubber (SBR)
| Parameter | Min | Max | Test Method |
|-----------|-----|-----|-------------|
| Mooney Viscosity (ML1+4 @ 100°C) | 45 | 55 | ASTM D1646 |
| Bound Styrene (%) | 22 | 25 | ASTM D5775 |
| Ash Content (%) | - | 0.3 | ASTM D5630 |

### Carbon Black (N330)
| Parameter | Min | Max | Test Method |
|-----------|-----|-----|-------------|
| Iodine Adsorption (mg/g) | 78 | 88 | ASTM D1510 |
| DBP Absorption (ml/100g) | 100 | 112 | ASTM D2414 |
| Moisture (%) | - | 1.0 | ASTM D1509 |

## Acceptance Criteria
- **Accept**: All parameters within specification
- **Conditional Accept**: Single parameter within 5% of limit, with engineering approval
- **Reject**: Any parameter outside specification, or multiple parameters at boundary

## Supplier Quality Rating
- A-grade (>98% pass rate): Standard inspection (1 sample per lot)
- B-grade (95-98% pass rate): Enhanced inspection (3 samples per lot)
- C-grade (<95% pass rate): Full inspection + supplier corrective action required
